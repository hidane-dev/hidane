hidane (火種, the seed of fire) — a Firestore emulator without Java.

A single Rust binary that replaces the official Cloud Firestore emulator. No JVM. Starts in milliseconds. Tested for parity against the official emulator.

Why hidane

21

Java 21 is now required

firebase-tools 15 dropped support for Java older than 21. Running a local database should not require a JDK.

Source: firebase-tools v15.0.0 release notes
0.74 s · 95 MiB

0.74 s to start, 95 MiB idle

The official emulator takes 0.74 s to open its port (2.5 s through the CLI) and sits at 95 MiB idle. hidane on main: 5.5 ms and 7.4 MiB.

Source: measurements and raw logs
11× slower

Writes slow down as data grows

With a listener attached, the official emulator's batch writes slow down in proportion to the number of documents: 11 times slower at 100k. hidane is designed so that a commit costs only what it changes: the same 100k documents take 4.9 s, flat per batch.

Source: measurements and raw logs

All figures are measurements, but not side by side: hidane's were taken later on a quieter machine, and a same-machine comparison is coming. Context: why hidane.

Parity

hidane treats the official emulator as the oracle: its answers are recorded, and the test suite replays every recording against hidane. No claim of compatibility without a test behind it.

So far: 17 recorded fixtures, from value ordering to the browser transport and export files, and SDK results compared side by side (results). A parity table will follow.

Known, intentional differences are listed in parity exceptions.
